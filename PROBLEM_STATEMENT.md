# Problem Statement

This document serves as the true north for the product. Every feature built for the MVP must directly address these points. If a feature does not solve this specific problem for this specific customer, it does not belong in the MVP.

## Who is the ideal customer?
Our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) consists of **B2B service providers (agencies, freelancers, consultants)** and **early-stage SaaS founders**. These are individuals or small teams who desperately need to acquire high-value B2B clients to grow, but lack the budget for a dedicated SDR (Sales Development Representative) team.

## What hurts the most?
The excruciating time and mental energy drain of **manual research and personalized writing**. 
They know that generic, high-volume "spray and pray" cold emails no longer work (and often ruin their domain reputation). However, doing it the "right way"—researching a prospect's company, finding the right decision-maker, and crafting a unique, highly relevant message—takes 15-30 minutes *per prospect*. It is unscalable for a small team.

## How do they solve it today?
They duct-tape together a fragmented workflow across multiple tabs:
1. Search LinkedIn Sales Navigator for companies.
2. Open company websites and read "About Us" or recent news to get context.
3. Search LinkedIn for the specific decision-maker.
4. Use a Chrome extension (like Hunter, Apollo, or Lusha) to guess or find their email.
5. Copy-paste all this context into ChatGPT, wrestling with prompts to get a decent email.
6. Copy the result into an email sequencer (like Lemlist or Instantly) or Gmail.

## Why is that bad?
- **Context Switching:** Jumping between 5 different tools destroys momentum and focus.
- **Error-Prone & Repetitive:** Manually moving data across tools leads to mistakes and burnout.
- **Inconsistent Quality:** Ad-hoc ChatGPT prompting often results in emails that still sound like a robot wrote them.
- **Unscalable:** At 20 minutes a lead, a founder can realistically only reach out to 15-20 people a day before running out of time, strangling their pipeline.

## Why will they pay?
They will pay because this tool provides **immediate, hard ROI**. It buys back hours of their time while directly driving revenue. If a $99/month subscription saves a consultant 20 hours a month of agonizing research and helps them land just one $5,000 contract, the software pays for itself 50x over instantly.

## What measurable outcome improves?
1. **Time-to-Outreach:** Reduced from ~20 minutes per lead to **< 1 minute**.
2. **Positive Reply Rate:** Increased from the industry average of 1-2% to **10-15%+** due to hyper-personalization and deep account research.

## What should NOT be in the MVP?
To maintain velocity and focus on the core value proposition (AI Research & Generation), the following are strictly **OUT OF SCOPE** for the MVP:
- ❌ **Native Email Sending/Sequencing:** Users can export data to CSV or copy-paste into their existing sequencer (Lemlist, Instantly). We are not building an email client.
- ❌ **CRM Functionality:** No deal pipelines, no task management, no revenue tracking.
- ❌ **Intent Data:** Tracking if a company is actively searching for a solution is too complex for now.
- ❌ **Multi-channel Automation:** No automated LinkedIn connection requests or Twitter DMs. Focus purely on email/text generation.
- ❌ **Browser Extensions:** Focus on a centralized web app workflow before expanding to surface-area plugins.
