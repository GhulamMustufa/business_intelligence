# LeadForge AI Design Notes

## Visual Identity: "Enterprise Slate"
The platform utilizes a sophisticated **Enterprise Slate** foundation designed for high-density, professional SaaS environments.

- **Primary Surface:** `#0b1326` (Deep Slate) – Chosen to reduce eye strain and provide a premium, technical backdrop.
- **Accent Color:** `#818cf8` (Mature Indigo) – Used for primary actions, progress indicators, and high-priority highlights.
- **Typography:** **Geist** – A high-performance sans-serif typeface optimized for data legibility and technical interfaces.
- **Hierarchy:** Subtle border treatments and low-saturation secondary accents create clear separation between intelligence modules without visual clutter.

## Core User Flow
The screens are organized to reflect the standard enterprise lead generation lifecycle:

1. **Access & Setup**
   - **Login:** Secure entry point with enterprise SSO support.
   - **Onboarding:** Role-based configuration to calibrate the AI engine.
   - **Workspace Dashboard:** Team-level overview and account health.

2. **Daily Command Center**
   - **Dashboard:** Personal intelligence console showing high-intent targets and credit usage.

3. **Lead Discovery & Intelligence**
   - **Lead Search:** High-density, three-pane discovery interface for granular filtering.
   - **Company Profile:** Deep-dive intelligence hub centralizing news, tech stack, and pain points.
   - **Contact Intelligence:** Individual prospect mapping with AI-driven outreach recommendations.

4. **Outreach & Management**
   - **AI Outreach Studio:** Collaborative multi-pane editor for personalized sequence generation.
   - **Saved Leads:** Organized workspace for folder management and bulk campaign staging.

5. **Platform Governance**
   - **Settings:** Centralized hub for profile, workspace configurations, and API management.
